Kalyanam Karoti

Kalyanam Karoti is one of the most trusted NGOs in India situated in Moradabad district of Uttar Pradesh. This is a National Awarded top charity organisation devoted for improving the ‘quality of life’ of the marginalised through its services in the domains of:

Eye Care

Aiming to eradicate curable blindness and providing best eye care solutions by Restoring Gift of Vision to the communities in need.

Learn more

Rehabilitation of the Disabled

Providing free Prosthetic and orthotic services including assistive devices to the amputees and disabled.

Learn more

Education for Children with Special Needs (CWSN)

Special School for intellectual/developmental disabled, hearing and visually impaired children.

Learn more

Outreach Services for Rural Health Development

Mobile healthcare system delivering best NGO services for the marginalised community residing in remote villages.

Learn more
